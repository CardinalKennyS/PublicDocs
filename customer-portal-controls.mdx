The existing Customer Portal is the control and evidence surface for the protected transaction lifecycle.

<Warning title="30-day extension">
  The Portal exists. Policy configuration, complete lifecycle audit, and expanded intelligence visibility are planned extensions. The browser is not the authoritative policy or risk engine.
</Warning>

## Planned controls

| Area | Portal responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Policies | Select and configure permitted rules and thresholds with version history. |
| Decisions | Display `ALLOW`, `REVIEW`, or `BLOCK` and each matched rule. |
| Intelligence | Display sanitized evidence, source, confidence, observation time, expiry, and degraded state. |
| Approvals | Record required human, counterparty, compliance, or multisig approvals. |
| Lifecycle | Correlate intent, decision, approval, contract action, and settlement outcome. |
| Audit | Provide tenant-scoped, tamper-evident records and controlled exports. |

## Security boundaries

- Keep provider credentials and raw restricted data off the client.
- Enforce tenant isolation and role-based access on the backend.
- Record the actor, time, policy version, reason, and previous value for configuration changes.
- Do not let a user-interface change overwrite historical decisions.
- Redact or hash sensitive evidence when retention or licensing terms require it.
