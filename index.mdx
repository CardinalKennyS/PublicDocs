Cardinal is building the trust, control, and evidence layer for digital asset transactions.

It helps a business check a payment before value moves, apply an `ALLOW`, `REVIEW`, or `BLOCK` policy, and retain an explainable record of the decision.

## Protection before value moves

<Steps>
  <Step title="Compose transaction intent">
    A wallet, application, or partner prepares the proposed transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Evaluate risk">
    Cardinal validates the request and evaluates available network, recipient, wallet, and contract signals.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return a clear decision">
    The Protection API returns `ALLOW`, `REVIEW`, or `BLOCK` with a risk score and explainable findings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue through a safer flow">
    The user continues normally, reviews the identified risk, or stops before signing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Cardinal is currently a connected MVP and controlled pilot environment. SafeSend uses a testnet flow, while production intelligence providers, mainnet deployment, external audits, and enterprise operations remain subject to verification.
</Warning>

## Built for organisations handling digital assets

Cardinal is designed for:

- businesses accepting digital asset payments, including property and other high-value transactions
- exchanges, payment platforms, and on-ramp or off-ramp providers
- custodians, treasury vaults, and institutional asset-control teams
- wallets, marketplaces, and tokenisation platforms

These are target use cases and integration pathways. Availability varies by product, network, asset, and partner configuration. Review [Product Status](/product-status) before representing a capability as production-ready.
